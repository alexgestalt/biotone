Biotone Visualizers

Five slow, evolving circle visualizers driven by a live sine tone. Tap ▶, then move the frequency and level faders. For relaxation and focus; for wellness only.

1 · Solar Corona
Flame tongues around a gold ring. Frequency sets the tongue count; level sets the flame length. 2 · Cymatic Plasma Halo
Standing-wave rings whose count follows the frequency. 3 · Fire Mandala
Flame petals whose symmetry order changes with the frequency. 4 · Nebula Portal
A breathing, domain-warped nebula. Frequency tightens the swirl. 5 · Particle Flame Ring
Embers drifting along curl-noise currents. Frequency shapes the ring lobes.
