Biotone Visualizers

Five Biotone ring visualizers driven by a live sine tone. Tap ▶, then move the frequency and level faders. For relaxation and focus; for wellness only.

1 · Corona
Slow flame tendrils orbit a dark core. The fader sets tendril count, length and speed. 2 · Lattice
Thin gold lines pulse like a magnetic field. The fader sets density and warp. 3 · Rise
Particle streams rise from the ring and fade. The fader sets emission, speed and warmth. 4 · Ripples
Concentric ripples move with the tone. The fader sets ripple count and pulse rate. 5 · Bloom
A breathing fractal bloom. The fader sets branch depth and breathing rate.
